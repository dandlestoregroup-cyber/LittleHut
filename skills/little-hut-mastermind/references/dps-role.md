# DPS — Design & Project Services

## One-line role

DPS is Little Hut's **experience-improvement function**: it turns property
observations into owner-approved design/project interventions that make a
Signature Moment more usable, memorable and commercially valuable.

## Position in the lifecycle

Qualification → **DPS improve** → BPS assess → Correct → Certify → Activate

DPS improves. BPS independently assesses and verifies.

## Workflow

Observation → Opportunity → Proposal → Owner Approval → Evidence → Impact

### Example

Observation:
Terrace is uncomfortable from 14:00–17:00 because of direct sun.

Opportunity:
Protect the "Barefoot afternoon" Moment.

Proposal:
Install a retractable shade.

Decision pack:
- Cost: EGP 18,000
- Guest impact: High
- Commercial impact: Medium
- Owner decision: Approved

Then:
installation evidence → visit/usage impact check → update Property Record.

## Authority boundary

DPS can design, recommend, scope, cost, coordinate and provide evidence.

DPS cannot:
- approve on behalf of the Owner;
- operate the home day to day;
- certify the intervention;
- grant BPS verification;
- grant the Little Hut seal;
- publish an unverified Moment claim.

Owner approves the intervention.
Operator supports/executes.
BPS checks standards and independently verifies.
